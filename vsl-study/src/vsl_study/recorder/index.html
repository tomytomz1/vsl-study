Record a browser tab

This page stays on your computer. Capture takes real playback time. Keep this computer awake, and keep VSL Study open.

This app cannot press play or notice when a video on another site ends. You stop it. A time limit, if you set one, is only a clock — not detected completion.

  1. Open the page
  2. Select the tab with audio
  3. Start and play
  4. Stop and process
  5. Open the evidence in VSL Study

The address is a note you typed, not proof that the selected tab was that page. Do not put passwords in it.

Prepare the player at the beginning and make the video large enough to read. In the browser picker, choose that tab and turn on sharing the tab’s sound. Do not share the whole screen if you can choose a tab. You can play a moment to confirm sound, then go back to the start if the player allows it.
